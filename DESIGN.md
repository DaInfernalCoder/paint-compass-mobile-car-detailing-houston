# Paint Compass design direction

## Subject, audience, and job

Mobile detailing for Houston drivers who need a clear direction based on the vehicle's current paint and cabin condition. The page should reduce guesswork and lead to calling or booking online once contact details are confirmed.

## Visual system

- Compass navy — `#17263A`
- Bearing blue — `#4D8FB8`
- Map cream — `#F8F5EC`
- Marker red — `#D95D39`
- Brass point — `#E7B44C`
- Display: Barlow Condensed
- Body: Manrope

## Layout thesis

The route frame behaves as a condition compass: assess, point to the useful scope, schedule, and review.

## Signature

A single compass needle replaces generic route arrows and points toward the next useful service decision.

## Critique

The first direction risked travel branding. The revision removes maps and locations; the compass appears only as a functional condition cue.
